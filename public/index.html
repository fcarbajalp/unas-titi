About Us

Handmade, one set at a time

Nails To Fit Art started with a paintbrush, a stack of blank tips, and friends asking where they could get a set like mine. Every design here is hand-painted to order — no printers, no factories, just practice and patience. It's a small, family-run shop, and each order is made specifically for you.

Behind the scenes

How it's made

From bare tips to ready-to-wear — every set goes through the same five steps.

1

Size & prep the tips against your actual nails

2

Base coat, then the tip colour goes on first

3

Hand-paint the pattern — stripes, dots, or foil detail

4

Seal with top coat so it holds up to daily wear

5

Glue on, press, hold — ready to go out the door
